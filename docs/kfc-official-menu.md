# Official KFC single-menu coverage and visible channel alternatives

The public `GetQueryFood` endpoint now supplies menus 5 (snacks/drinks) and 184 (egg tarts), independently of coupon discovery. The reader calls the same anonymous official API as the public menu page. It retains the reference shop TWI104, fulfilment, official meal period, listed fixed price, campaign start/end, and sold-out state. The five official periods are looked up through `GetMealPeriodID`, not inferred from one day's current clock.

The current reviewed snapshot has 64 distinct fixed-price products across takeaway/delivery and their actual meal periods. A small typed food map shares only literal, unambiguous quantities with coupon components. Named snack boxes/platters remain one named pack; marketing paragraphs never become guessed counts of chicken or other components. Unknown/new names get distinct named-pack identities. Sauces/drinks remain selectable but do not satisfy snack variety or snack portions. A required fixed ingredient can be fulfilled by eligible coupon offers or official standalone offers together.

Successful refreshes remove disappeared entries. Failed page/period refreshes keep their original timestamps and do not extend validity. Both the current time and requested dining time must stay inside an eight-day observation window. The official product end date is also applied; an administrative rolling end date alone does not imply that a product is marketed as limited edition. Refresh joins the existing weekly Sunday schedule. Applying either brand's menu cache preserves the other brand's standalone offers.

Brand/date/payment/membership constraints continue to apply to food discovery. When a food has a priced takeaway option but none under the selected fulfilment, its main/snack card stays visible with an explicit "改用外帶選擇" action. That action switches the channel before adding the food; it never presents a takeaway price as delivery or changes the channel merely by rendering the page. A Pizza Hut-only choice still excludes KFC food. Off-time snacks remain visible with their actual alternative window.
